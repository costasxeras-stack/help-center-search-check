# Product brief

## Problem

Help center search often shows an article that does not answer the question, and people contact support anyway. Teams want to know whether AI search would fix this and when search should admit it has no good answer.

## Users

- **Help center and support product managers** deciding whether to change search.
- **Content teams** deciding what to write and how to title articles.
- **Hiring managers** reading this as an example of how I evaluate search.

## Goals

- Measure how often a good article comes first and in the top 3, for keyword, AI and hybrid search.
- Show where search fails, not just the average.
- Test whether a score can decide when to show "no good answer".
- Be honest about what the test data can and cannot show.

## Non-goals for 0.1.0

- A live search box. The page shows results computed in advance.
- Tuning or training models.
- Real search logs. None are public with ratings.

## Success measures

- Every number on the page is reproduced by the scripts from a clean folder.
- Findings hold in both scoring views, or say clearly when they don't.
- A support PM can name one change to try after reading the page: [feedback from a reader, to collect].
