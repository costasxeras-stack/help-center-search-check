# 0004: Open with the search widget

**Status:** Accepted · 23 November 2026

## Context

Version 0.1.0 opened with three number tiles and put the example questions at the bottom of the page. It shared one visual template with the other portfolio apps, and a visitor had to scroll past tables to see what a customer would actually get.

## Decision

Open with a help-center search widget. A real question from the test set sits in the search field, typed out once on page load, and the top 3 articles from keyword, AI and hybrid search sit under it with the people's rating on each. "Show another question" moves through the 60 sampled questions. On phones the three methods are tabs, with the hybrid shown first.

The headline numbers follow as one row of short findings. The method table and charts stay below.

The app gets its own look: help-desk violet, Figtree and round corners.

## Consequences

- A visitor judges search the way a customer meets it, before reading any number.
- The typing effect runs once and is skipped when the visitor has asked for reduced motion.
